# Pricing

Two kinds of price exist in the catalogue.

- **Individual candles** (category `candles`): the owner sets the selling price. The system suggests it from the candle's weight, its recipe and the owner's settings. A suggestion is never applied automatically.
- **Other products** (bouquets, gift sets, diffusers, event favors, accessories): unchanged. They keep their documented rules in `PricingService`.

A selling price is only written in three ways: the owner approves a suggestion, the owner types a price, or one of the other products' rules recalculates it. Recalculating costs never changes a candle's price.

## Candle prices

### Inputs

- **Finished weight** (grams, `products.candle_weight_grams`). It starts at the recipe total. The recipe is scaled to the weight.
- **Recipe** (`product_materials`, recipe type `ACTUAL`). Each line's grams come from the recipe.
- **Material unit costs** (`materials.unit_cost`, per gram, or per millilitre for fragrance). Purchase prices and their history are in `material_purchase_prices`.
- **Settings** (`pricing_settings`, editable on the Products page).

### Formula

```
material grams   = recipe grams x (weight / recipe total)
fragrance grams  -> millilitres = grams / density (g/ml)
materials cost   = sum(grams x unit cost)
known unit cost  = materials cost + packaging per candle (1 TND)
retail price     = RoundUp(known unit cost x 2.10, 0.50 TND)
minimum price    = known unit cost x 1.40        (40% markup on total cost)
unit price (n)   = RoundUp(minimum + (retail - minimum) / n^0.23, 0.05 TND), never below minimum
```

For quantity 1 the unit price is the retail price. The unit price falls smoothly as the quantity grows, and it never goes below the minimum. Rounding is always upward. There are no fixed quantity tiers.

Example: the Peony candle at 75 g. Materials 4.635 TND, plus 1 TND packaging, gives 5.635 TND known cost. Retail is 5.635 × 2.10 = 11.834, rounded up to **12.000 TND**. Minimum is 7.889 TND.

### Owner settings

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| `retail_multiplier` | 2.10 |
| `candle_minimum_markup_percent` | 40 |
| `quantity_price_exponent` | 0.23 |
| `retail_rounding_tnd` | 0.50 |
| `quantity_rounding_tnd` | 0.05 |
| `packaging_per_candle_tnd` | 1 |
| `density_g_per_ml` | 1 (provisional) |
| `fragrance_density_confirmed` | 0 (not confirmed) |

Settings are range-checked before they are saved. Changing them changes suggestions only.

### Approval rules

- **Approve suggestion**: sets the selling price to the suggested retail price. Single or bulk (the Products page lists every candle with its current price and suggestion).
- **Manual price**: the owner types a price. It must be at least the minimum price, so the 40% markup is protected.
- Every change is recorded in `product_price_history` with the previous and new price and source.
- Source values: `manual`, `calculated`, and `automatic_incorrect` (the price was generated by the old cost × 2 or × 2.5 rules; it is not valid).

### Provisional costs

Labour and overhead are not recorded, so every known cost is labelled provisional. Fragrance is provisional until its density is confirmed. The fragrance is bought in millilitres and weighed in grams, and the conversion uses the density setting.

### Activation

A candle can be activated only when its selling price is set by the owner (approved or typed). An automatic price from the old rules does not count.

## Material purchase prices

Migration `035_candle_pricing_integration.sql` records the owner's purchase prices in `material_purchase_prices`:

| Material | Purchase price | Unit cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soy wax | 40 TND/kg | 0.040 TND/g |
| Beeswax | 50 TND/kg | 0.050 TND/g |
| Stearic acid | 22 TND/kg | 0.022 TND/g |
| Fragrance oil | 25 TND/100 ml | 0.250 TND/ml (provisional density) |

Earlier purchase prices were not recorded, so no earlier prices or effective dates were invented (`effective_from` is NULL). Stock quantities are not changed. Completed orders keep their own cost and price snapshots (`order_item_materials`, `order_items`).

## Code

- `src/Services/CandlePricing.php`: the pure calculation (integer arithmetic, no database).
- `src/Services/CandlePriceService.php`: reads the candle's data, calculates the suggestion and the quantity table, and applies approvals and manual prices.
- `src/Repositories/CandlePricingRepository.php`: reads and writes the candle price data.
- `src/Services/PricingService.php`: recalculates costs for every product. Candle selling prices are never written here.
- `src/Controllers/AdminProductController.php`: the actions `save_candle_weight`, `apply_suggested_price`, `set_manual_price`, `apply_suggested_prices` and `save_pricing_settings`.
- `app/Views/Pages/admin/catalogue/product-edit.php`: the Price section for one candle.
- `app/Views/Pages/admin/catalogue/products.php`: the comparison table, bulk approval, and pricing settings.

## Tests

- `tests/Unit/verify-candle-pricing.php`: the calculation, including the 75 g example, quantities, and rejected inputs.
- `tests/Integration/verify-candle-pricing.php`: approval, manual price minimum, history, recalculation not overwriting prices, settings changes, and bulk approval, against a disposable database.

## Not done yet

- Sets and diffusers still use the old cost × 2 rule, which the spec does not cover. They need the owner's decision.
- Event favors still use their own tier multipliers. The spec's quantity formula is not applied to them.
- The old margin settings (`margin_*`, `waste_percent`, `rounding_step_tnd`, `minimum_margin_warning_percent`) remain in `pricing_settings` but no longer drive any price. They can be removed later.
- Labour, overhead, wicks and pigments (not recorded yet).
- The bouquet quantity-tier feature (`product_quantity_prices`) still exists; the spec asks for no fixed tiers.
