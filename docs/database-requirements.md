# DATABASE REQUIREMENTS

## CATEGORIES

The shop has six product categories:

### Candles

- Made to order
- Can use the normal cart workflow
- Customer selects one color and one scent
- Has one main image and one image for every available color

### Candle Sets

- Created and managed by the admin
- Contains at least two candle products
- May also contain accessories
- Customer selects one color and one scent for the entire set
- Displays its component products, quantities, prices, and product-page links
- Can use the normal cart workflow

### Bouquets

- Made to order
- Cannot be added to the normal cart
- Budget, packaging, color, and scent are confirmed manually
- Stores the number of candles separately from the description
- Admin creates the order after reaching an agreement with the customer

### Event Favors

- Made to order
- Minimum quantity is 10
- Cannot be added to the normal cart
- Budget, packaging, color, scent, and quantity are confirmed manually
- Stores the number of candles separately from the description
- Admin creates the order after reaching an agreement with the customer

### Home Diffusers

- Made to order
- Can use the normal cart workflow
- Customer selects one color and one scent
- Has its own material recipe and real production cost
- Its displayed price is calculated using candle-equivalent pricing

### Accessories

- Genuine products with their own category and product pages
- Managed using stock quantities
- Cannot be purchased alone
- Must accompany a candle or candle set
- Can be requested when out of stock
- Uses colors but does not use scents


## PRODUCT TYPES

Available product types include:

- Flowers
- Bubble
- In container
- Bouquet
- Wax melt
- Other
- Accessories

### Product-type rules

- Every individual product has one product type.
- A candle set appears under every product type represented by its component products.
- A set’s type visibility updates automatically when its components change.


## PRODUCT INFORMATION

Every product can have:

- Product ID
- Name and description translations
- Category
- Product type
- Actual total cost
- Calculated selling price
- Calculated net profit
- Active/inactive status
- Main image path
- Available colors and their image paths
- Available scents when applicable
- Product material requirements when applicable

The Product ID is generated by the system.

Net profit is calculated from the selling price and actual total cost. It should not be maintained manually.

Product material requirements are represented through a product recipe/BOM relationship rather than a single field.


## MULTILINGUAL PRODUCT CONTENT

- Admin enters the product name and description in one language.
- The system automatically generates the other language versions.
- English, French, and Arabic versions are stored.
- Admin can review and edit the generated translations.


## PRODUCT RECIPES AND MATERIAL STOCK

- Materials are classified as raw materials, cards, floral paper, boxes, protective packaging, labor, overhead, or tools.
- Consumable types are deducted through production recipes; reusable tools may be stock-tracked but are not consumed by recipes.
- Admin manages the materials and quantities required to produce each product.
- Materials may include raw materials and packaging.
- Labor and other production expenses are included when calculating total cost.
- Material costs automatically affect the current product cost and selling price.
- Material stock is reduced when an order becomes `prepared`.
- Required material quantity is multiplied by the ordered product quantity.
- Material stock is reduced only once per order.
- Moving an order away from `prepared` and back again must not deduct the materials twice.
- Every stock addition, order deduction, restock, manual adjustment, damage, or waste event is recorded as an immutable material stock movement.


## ACTIVE/INACTIVE BEHAVIOR

- Active products appear publicly and can be ordered or requested according to their category rules.
- Inactive products are hidden from customers and cannot be newly ordered or requested.
- Inactive products remain available to administrators and historical orders.
- Incomplete products can be saved as inactive drafts.
- Products can become active only after satisfying their activation requirements.

### Universal activation requirements

Every active product must have:

- Name and description in all three languages
- Category
- Product type
- Valid selling price
- Main image

### Candle activation requirements

An active candle must have:

- Complete material recipe/BOM
- At least one available color
- At least one available scent
- One image for every available color

### Candle-set activation requirements

An active candle set must:

- Contain at least two candle products
- Have active required candle components
- Have quantities for its components
- Have at least one available color
- Have at least one available scent
- Have one image for every available color

### Bouquet and Event Favor activation requirements

- Color and scent options do not need to be selected on the website.
- Customization is agreed manually with the customer.
- These products use the request-only workflow.

### Home-diffuser activation requirements

An active home diffuser must have:

- Complete diffuser recipe/BOM for material usage and actual cost
- Enough candle-equivalent information to calculate its displayed price
- At least one available color
- At least one available scent
- One image for every available color

### Accessory activation requirements

An active accessory must have:

- Original purchase cost
- Stock quantity; zero is allowed
- At least one available color
- One image for every available color
- No scent requirement


## MADE-TO-ORDER AND STOCK BEHAVIOR

- All categories except Accessories are made after order confirmation.
- Accessories are kept in stock.
- An active in-stock accessory can accompany a candle or set.
- An active out-of-stock accessory can be requested.
- An inactive accessory can neither be purchased nor requested.


## IMAGE REQUIREMENTS

- Admin adds and manages product images through the admin dashboard.
- Every product has one main image.
- The main image is selected to match the website’s visual style.
- Every available product color has exactly one image.
- Selecting a color changes the displayed image.
- Selecting a scent does not change the displayed image.
- The database stores relative image paths, not image files.

Images are organized as:

`public/assets/products/{category}/{product}/`

Example database paths:

- `assets/products/candles/bubble-candle/main.jpg`
- `assets/products/candles/bubble-candle/pink.jpg`
- `assets/products/candles/bubble-candle/white.jpg`


## SCENT AND COLOR BEHAVIOR

- Color and scent are independent options.
- Selecting a color does not restrict the selected scent.
- Selecting a scent does not restrict the selected color.
- Selecting a color changes the displayed product image.
- Selecting a scent does not change the displayed image.
- Scent availability is managed according to fragrance-material stock.

For Candles, Candle Sets, and Home Diffusers:

- Customer must select a color and scent before adding the product to the cart.

For Bouquets and Event Favors:

- Color and scent are agreed manually during the request process.
- They are not selected through the normal cart.

For Accessories:

- Customer selects a color.
- Scent selection is not required.


## BASE-PRICE RULES

### General cost rules

Total cost includes:

- Raw materials
- Packaging
- Labor
- Other production expenses

Current product costs and selling prices recalculate automatically when their cost inputs change.

Net profit is calculated as:

`Net profit = selling price - actual total cost`

### Candles

`Selling price = actual total cost × 2`

### Candle Sets

Candle and other production components receive the ×2 markup.

Accessories are added at their original cost without the ×2 markup.

`Set price = (non-accessory component costs × 2) + accessory costs`

This rule may change later.

### Bouquets

`Base price = sum of component total costs × 2`

The final product details and price are confirmed manually with the customer.

This rule may change later.

### Event Favors

`Batch price = unit total cost × quantity × tier multiplier`

Quantity rules:

- Fewer than 10 cannot be ordered
- 10–19 items: total batch cost × 1.75
- 20–39 items: total batch cost × 1.5
- 40 or more items: total batch cost × 1.3

The candle’s real production cost is used before applying the quantity multiplier.

### Home Diffusers

- The database calculates the diffuser’s actual cost from its real recipe.
- The actual diffuser cost is used for inventory and profit calculations.
- The displayed selling price uses candle-equivalent pricing.
- The candle-equivalent calculation uses the same shape and size with the candle wax percentages.
- If an equivalent candle product exists, the diffuser displays the same selling price.
- Candle and diffuser profits may differ because their actual recipes and costs differ.

### Accessories

- Accessories use their original purchase cost.
- Accessories do not receive an individual ×2 markup.
- When added to a candle set, their cost is added after calculating the marked-up candle components.


## EXISTING ORDER PRICE RULES

- Changes to material costs automatically update current catalogue prices.
- Existing order prices never change silently.
- Before an order becomes `prepared`, its price may change only after contacting the customer and receiving agreement.
- From `prepared` onward, the agreed order price is permanently locked.


## ORDER WORKFLOWS

### Order statuses

Orders may use these statuses:

1. Requested
2. Confirmed
3. Preparing
4. Prepared
5. Waiting for pickup
6. Picked up
7. In delivery
8. Delivered

### Normal cart orders

Candles, Candle Sets, and Home Diffusers use the normal cart workflow.

- Minimum normal order total is 40 DT.
- Accessories cannot be purchased alone.
- Accessories must accompany a candle or candle set.

For orders from 40 DT to less than 100 DT:

- Customer provides contact details.
- Customer provides the required date.
- Customer sees information about preparation time before confirmation.

For orders of 100 DT or more:

- A deposit of at least 40% is required.
- Customer provides contact details and the required date.
- The business contacts the customer to confirm the order.

### Bouquet and Event Favor requests

- Bouquets and Event Favors cannot be added to the normal cart.
- Their product pages provide Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, and TikTok contact options.
- Customers may contact the business directly.
- Customers may alternatively submit their contact details for a callback.
- Budget, packaging availability, color, scent, quantity, and required date are agreed manually.
- Admin creates the order after reaching an agreement with the customer.
